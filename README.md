# Desk to Day Planner

An ADHD-friendly planner built around the Desk-to-Day method: **Drop it, Sort it, Slot it.** Plan just enough, just in time.

Open the planner: https://stephaniekaiser.github.io/desk-to-day/

On an iPad or iPhone, open the link in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app.

Your tasks are saved only in the browser on your own device. Nobody else, including the person who shared this link, can see them. Use **Settings → Your data** to back up or move your planner.
